# The 1885 Post — Colleague Guide

The 1885 Post lives at **https://newsletters.evmed.app**. It is an Arizona State
University newsletter archive.

## Get your newsletter into the archive

Send (or CC/BCC) your newsletter to **newsletters@evmed.app** when you send it out. It
shows up on the homepage automatically within moments — no extra steps. Unsubscribe /
manage-preferences links are automatically disabled in the archived copy (so the public
archive can't be used to unsubscribe someone); everything else, including all your
regular content links, is preserved exactly as sent.

## Add a "recent newsletters" widget to your department website

Any of the newsletters in the archive can be embedded as a small, public widget on
another website — no login required for people viewing it, and clicking a newsletter
opens the full thing. Any logged-in archive user can create one, no admin access needed:

1. Go to **https://newsletters.evmed.app/embeds** (also linked as "Embeds" at the top of
   every page).
2. Fill in a name, a sender email to filter to (leave blank to show newsletters from
   *all* senders), how many to show (0 shows all of them), and sort order, then
   **Create embed**.
3. Click **Copy iframe code** next to it and paste that directly into your website's
   HTML.

You can revisit `/embeds` any time to **Edit** an embed you created (updates what it shows
without breaking the link you already pasted somewhere) or **Revoke** it (immediately
stops it from working anywhere it's embedded). Editing or revoking an embed someone else
created requires admin access for that embed's sender.

## Don't have admin access yet?

Admin access is per sending address — it lets you delete newsletters from that sender in
the archive, backdate them (useful for backfilling old issues), and edit/revoke embeds
scoped to that sender even if you didn't create them. It's not needed just to create your
own embeds. See **https://newsletters.evmed.app/permissions** ("Permissions" in the
header) for a read-only list of who currently administers which sender.
