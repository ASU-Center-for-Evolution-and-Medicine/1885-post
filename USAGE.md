# Newsletter Archive — Colleague Guide

The archive lives at **https://newsletters.evmed.app**.

## Get your newsletter into the archive

Send (or CC/BCC) your newsletter to **newsletters@evmed.app** when you send it out. It
shows up on the homepage automatically within moments — no extra steps. Unsubscribe /
manage-preferences links are automatically disabled in the archived copy (so the public
archive can't be used to unsubscribe someone); everything else, including all your
regular content links, is preserved exactly as sent.

## Add a "recent newsletters" widget to your department website

Any of your recent newsletters can be embedded as a small, public widget on another
website — no login required for people viewing it, and clicking a newsletter opens the
full thing.

If you already have admin access for your sending address:

1. Go to **https://newsletters.evmed.app/admin**.
2. Under **Embeds**, fill in a name, your sender email (leave blank only if you want
   *all* senders' newsletters, not just yours), how many to show (0 shows all of them),
   and sort order, then **Create embed**.
3. Click **Copy iframe code** next to it and paste that directly into your website's
   HTML.

You can revisit `/admin` any time to **Edit** an embed (updates what it shows without
breaking the link you already pasted somewhere) or **Revoke** it (immediately stops it
from working anywhere it's embedded).

## Don't have admin access yet?

Admin access is per sending address — it lets you delete your own newsletters from the
archive, backdate them (useful for backfilling old issues), and create/manage embeds for
your address, without giving you any control over anyone else's newsletters.

**Contact Suhail (suhail.ghafoor@asu.edu)** to be set up as an admin for your sending
address.
